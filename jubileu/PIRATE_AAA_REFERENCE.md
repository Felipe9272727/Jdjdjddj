# AAA Pirate-Captain Reference Dossier
*Benchmark material for the model critic. Our captain (Floor 7) must be judged AGAINST these named AAA exemplars, not in a vacuum. Every score should be anchored to "AAA does X, ours does Y, the gap is Z."*

Sources: Rare / Sea of Thieves dev breakdowns, Ubisoft AC4 *Black Flag* (Edward Kenway), ArtStation game-ready pirate-captain specs.

---

## EXEMPLAR A — Sea of Thieves pirates (Rare) — the STYLIZED AAA bar
Even though SoT is stylized (not realistic), it is the gold standard for *characterful, hand-crafted-looking* pirates. What makes them AAA:

- **Hierarchical face construction.** Two layers: (1) section-level transforms that rotate/scale/translate whole face regions (brow, cheek, jaw, nose mass), then (2) feature-level shapes. Noses come in distinct sculpted families: *pointy with tight nostrils*, *rounded with a curved tip*, *square with slanted nostrils*. Eyes range from *barely-open slits* to *wide and round*. Lips are *full* or *absent*. — Our face must have this kind of deliberate, sculpted regional structure, not generic spheres.
- **Micro-character details** are mandatory: gammy/asymmetric eyes, freckles, **scars crossing the brow or eye**, tattoos, weather-beaten skin, peg legs, eyepatches. A bare pirate face reads as unfinished.
- **No feature interpenetration** — lips/eyes never clip through the nose; forms are seated, not floating.
- **Asymmetry & tonal variation in the skin** — never one flat skin value; cheeks/nose ruddier, brow/eye-sockets darker.
- **Silhouette reads as "a person who lived a hard life"** even at a thumbnail.

## EXEMPLAR B — Edward Kenway, AC4 *Black Flag* (Ubisoft) — the REALISTIC AAA bar
- **Performance-capture-grade face** with viseme + emotion presets; dedicated **jaw and eye bones**.
- **Beard is its own bodygroup** with real volume and a clean hairline transition into skin — not a smooth bib stuck on the chin.
- **Layered costume telling a story**: tailored coat *over* a shirt, a wide belt + baldric/sash crossing the chest, holsters, bracers/greaves, leather pieces visibly *crafted and upgraded* (material layering you can read). The coat reads as **tailored = authority**.
- **Cloth simulation** on the coat skirt/cloak — secondary motion, weight, follow-through.

## EXEMPLAR C — ArtStation game-ready stylized pirate captain — the HARD SPEC bar
Concrete numbers an AAA hero pirate hits:
- **~27,400 verts / ~31,500 tris** (dense enough for rounded forms — no faceted spheres).
- **10× 4K (4096²) texture sets, 11 PBR materials.**
- **PBR maps**: albedo + normal + roughness + metallic + AO (+ often height/SSS). Roughness VARIES across a surface (worn vs fresh).
- **52 Live-Link face blendshapes**, jaw + eye controls.
- Costume rule: **a formal/military coat contrasted with loose, "spotty" trousers** = a man who lived outside the law; **tricorne carries a symbol/badge**; **beard & hair share analogous hues** for cohesion.

## THE AAA CHECKLIST (score our captain against THIS)
**Proportions / silhouette**
- 7–7.5 head-heights tall; shoulders clearly broader than hips; a V-torso with a sternum/clavicle read; hands roughly face-sized with weight.
- Reads in **pure black silhouette** — you can name him as "pirate captain" from the outline alone.

**Face (where AAA is won or lost)**
- Sculpted regional structure (brow ridge that overhangs + shadows, eye sockets recessed, cheekbone PLANES, a nose with bridge/wings, a mouth, a jaw line). No floating spheres.
- Asymmetry + a scar / weathering; ruddy/darker tonal zones; eyes with a real iris-to-white ratio.

**Costume / material**
- **3+ readable material zones** layered (coat / waistcoat / shirt / belt / leather), each with its OWN albedo, roughness, and finish — not one flat colour.
- Edge wear, salt-stain, dirt in the creases; fabric weave on cloth; leather grain; scratched/worn metal; gold that's warm not olive.
- Gilt trim, buttons, buckle, baldric — period costume density.

**Hair / beard / hat**
- Beard with clumping, a forked/tapered shape, and a clean hairline (not a smooth dome). Hair shows lock shapes. Tricorne crisp with a badge/cockade.

**Hands**
- Both equally articulated; palm wraps, fingers taper and fan, a thumb opposes. Not paddles with sausages.

> The critic must demand AAA. "Better than last round" is NOT a pass — the bar is *would this ship next to Edward Kenway / a Sea of Thieves pirate?*
